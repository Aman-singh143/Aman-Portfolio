About

I’m a Data Engineer at ParallelDots, focused on making operational data useful, reliable, and ready for the people who need to act on it. I work across ETL, data organization, backend systems, reporting, and automation.

My approach is consultative: understand the business process first, then choose the simplest dependable solution. A robust data flow or automation is often more valuable than forcing AI into a problem; when AI is useful, I integrate it deliberately.

What I Do

  • Build and improve Python ETL pipelines that turn raw inputs into usable analytics outputs
  • Connect data sources, APIs, databases, and reporting layers
  • Design validation and data-quality checks for reliable workflows
  • Develop FastAPI/Flask services and focused internal tools
  • Automate recurring reporting, notifications, and operational tasks
  • Optimize SQL workflows and database access for business-facing applications
  • Assess where AI can improve document, classification, search, or extraction workflows
